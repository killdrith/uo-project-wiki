Half-Plate Chest

The current half-plate armor with the approved tighter torso, darker chest materials, broad upper-left lighting and dark material-tinted outlines with gaps on lit edges.

35 animations · 1,050 unique sprites · five stored directions. Previews show the game-encoded sprites over the original UO male body and gray horse. The remaining three directions are mirrored by the client.

A separate server item. Spawn with [add HalfPlateChest and equip it on a male human. The original platemail chest is restored; compare it with [add PlateChest. No server restart is needed for this art update. The backpack icon and male paperdoll use the reviewed custom half-plate art; the female paperdoll retains the stock fallback.

The torso is 10% narrower and 5% shallower, with darker torso materials to distinguish the shoulder pads. Your saved poses, rigid metal and flexible leather connections are retained. Metal receives hues; leather and dark outlines retain their colors. The body wears only the chest armor in these previews.
