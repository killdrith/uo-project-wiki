War Axe — variable outline animation review

The War Axe's variable exterior outline now follows the opaque pixels UO actually keeps, eliminating the transparent fringe that previously separated the axe from its outline. Highlight-pixel omissions are disabled (previously a small number of strongly illuminated edge pixels were omitted). It remains darker on shadow-facing edges, lighter on illuminated edges, tinted by its painted surface colors, one pixel maximum width, with no random noise. These previews use the latest approved poses, including Combat Walk, and retain the body occlusion and mounted horse cutouts.

22 animations · 605 unique sprites · five stored directions. The previews show sprites after UO color/transparency encoding, over the original male body and gray horse. All 605 views passed the opaque edge adjacency check.
