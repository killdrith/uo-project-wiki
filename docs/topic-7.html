DESIGN & ARCHITECTURE / SEPTEMBER 2026

Creature ai and encounter behavior

Working notes · Preferences, recommendations, and exploration retain their original status. Technical research requires version-specific verification.

Jim asked whether Outlands uses reworked AI and whether ModernUO behavior is modifiable.

Outlands' own overview describes custom creature AI and behaviors, with examples including thrown attacks, death explosions, and enemies working together. Patch notes document further creature-ability changes. This supports custom behavior; it does not establish how much of the underlying AI framework was replaced.

ModernUO's inspected development documentation describes standard AI categories and custom AI assignment. Movement and decision-making belong in AI classes, while additional content behavior can use creature hooks and carefully timed logic.

Relative effort discussed:
- Aggression range, movement pace, or attack timing: small changes.
- Special attacks, low-health enrages, death effects: small to moderate.
- Maintaining distance, ally healing, role-based targeting: moderate.
- Boss phases, summons, hazards, telegraphs: moderate to substantial.
- Coordinated flanking, retreats, reinforcements, and reliable complex navigation: substantial.

A fighter/archer/healer/scout bandit group was an illustrative reusable behavior concept, not selected content.

Design challenges include stuck creatures, target thrashing, unfun retreats, pathfinding cost, and excessive repeated searches. Consequential actions need actual cooldowns; a callback occurring more frequently must not grant extra attacks. Spatially bounded queries and sensible decision intervals matter.
